# Base rate fallacy

- base rate is people who get a diagnosis from a mental health professional, maybe more women do that

# Selection bias

- Maybe the men who seek out mental health professionals are the ones who get a diagnosis
- Need to think about this more

# Priming effects

- in their training, mental health professionals learn about the gender difference and are thus more likely to find confirmatory evidence of it because they've been primed to see it
