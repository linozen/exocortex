> Ideological polarization is generated as a side-effect of content recommendation algorithms optimizing for user engagement and advertising revenues.

— [Concilience Project glossary](https://consilienceproject.org/glossary/)

The mechanism is not conspiracy but optimization. Engagement metrics correlate with emotional activation; emotional activation correlates with content that confirms identity or attacks opposing identity; recommender systems learn to surface that content. The platform doesn't intend radicalization — radicalization is what falls out of optimizing for time-on-platform.

The structural difficulty: even an operator who wanted to remove the effect couldn't simply "turn it off" without sacrificing the metric the business depends on. The harm is endogenous to the business model.

## Why this matters beyond "social media is bad"

- It corrodes the [[epistemic-commons]] — shared discourse is replaced by tribally-segmented outrage feeds
- It interacts with [[turnkey-tyranny|surveillance infrastructure]] — the same profile data that personalizes ads personalizes radicalization
- It selects for the loudest, most emotionally activating voices in any population, regardless of expertise
- It makes [[sensemaking]] at scale harder: each tribe sees a different, internally consistent picture of reality
