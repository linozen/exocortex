> It is both the public spaces — and private spaces — where people come together to pursue a mutual understanding of issues critical to their society.

— [Concilience Project glossary](https://consilienceproject.org/glossary/)

The epistemic commons is a [[commons|commons resource]] in the same structural sense as a fishery or pasture: it provides a shared good that requires shared stewardship, and it can be over-exploited or polluted.

What pollutes it:

- **Engagement-optimized algorithms** that surface emotionally activating rather than epistemically nourishing content
- **Microtargeting and information warfare** that turns the commons into a marketing-and-manipulation channel
- **Sensationalism and outrage cycles** that crowd out longer-form thinking
- **Thought-terminating clichés** that stop further questioning
- **Epistemic hubris** posing as certainty in domains that demand humility

The Concilience Project's recurring framing: rebuilding a functioning epistemic commons is a precondition for addressing any other civilizational-scale problem. Without it, even technically correct solutions cannot be coordinated on.
