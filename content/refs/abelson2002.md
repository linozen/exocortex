# SICP — Abelson & Sussman, 2002

The classic MIT introductory programming text, taught in Scheme. Originally written for 6.001; the 2002 edition is the second edition. Freely available at [mitp-content-server.mit.edu/books/content/sectbyfn/books_pres_0/6515/sicp.zip](https://web.mit.edu/6.001/6.037/sicp.pdf) and online at [sarabander.github.io/sicp](https://sarabander.github.io/sicp/). Exercise solutions: [community.schemewiki.org/?SICP-Solutions](http://community.schemewiki.org/?SICP-Solutions).

## Status

Worked through Chapter 1 (Building Abstractions with Procedures) and the first sections of Chapter 2 (Building Abstractions with Data). Did not continue to Chapter 3 (Modularity, Objects, State), Chapter 4 (Metalinguistic Abstraction — including the metacircular evaluator), or Chapter 5 (Computing with Register Machines).

## What was covered

- §1.1 — elements of programming, substitution model, conditional expressions, block structure
- §1.2 — recursion vs iteration, orders of growth, exponentiation, GCD, primality
- §1.3 — higher-order procedures, lambda, procedures as general methods, procedures as returned values
- §2.1 (beginning) — data abstraction, pairs, abstraction barriers
