# Full Stack Open — University of Helsinki

A self-paced MOOC by the University of Helsinki on modern full-stack web development. Free, regularly updated, university credit available to Finnish residents. Source: [fullstackopen.com](https://fullstackopen.com/en/#course-contents).

## Status

Completed parts 0 (Fundamentals of Web Apps), 1 (Intro to React), 2 (Communicating with Server), and 3 (Programming a Server with NodeJS and Express). Did not continue past part 3 — parts 4 (testing), 5 (testing React), 6 (Redux), 7 (Router/hooks/CSS), 8 (GraphQL), 9 (TypeScript), 10 (React Native), 11 (CI/CD), 12 (Containers), 13 (relational DBs) remain unworked. I stopped because the JavaScript/TypeScript ecosystem is fast moving and I had the feeling that I learned more working actual projects.

## What was covered

- Part 0 — HTTP, the DOM, forms, AJAX, the SPA transition, JS library landscape
- Part 1 — JSX, components, props, state, `useState`, event handlers, hook discipline
- Part 2 — `useEffect`, server communication (`fetch`/`axios`), controlled forms, conditional rendering, basic styling
- Part 3 — Express routing, REST resource design, middleware, MongoDB via Mongoose, deployment, error handling
